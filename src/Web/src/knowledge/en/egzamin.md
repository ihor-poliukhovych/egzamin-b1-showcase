# How the B1 exam works

> Five parts, each with its own timer and its own 50% threshold: what to expect on the exam, how the oral part is organised, and how to prepare for the day itself.

## What this exam is

**Egzamin certyfikatowy z języka polskiego jako obcego** is the state exam in Polish as a foreign language. It is organised by the **Państwowa Komisja ds. Poświadczania Znajomości Języka Polskiego jako Obcego**, which also publishes the official task collections and sample arkusze. A B1 certificate is often needed for a karta pobytu, EU long-term resident status or citizenship.

The exam does not test "Polish in general". It tests five specific skills in a fixed format. That is why it pays to prepare by format: know the task types, the timing and what costs you points.

## The five parts in order

The parts always come in the same order. The timings and points below are taken from a real arkusz (czerwiec 2024).

| No. | Part | Time | Points | Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rozumienie ze słuchu | 30 min | 30 | 15 |
| 2 | Rozumienie tekstów pisanych | 40 min | 30 | 15 |
| 3 | Poprawność gramatyczna | 45 min | 30 | 15 |
| 4 | Pisanie | 75 min | 30 | 15 |
| 5 | Mówienie | about 20 min | 40 | 20 |

### 1. Rozumienie ze słuchu

Five tasks: short utterances (where is this said, who is speaking), short dialogues, an interview, TAK/NIE statements, and matching speakers to opinions. An important detail: **the first task is played only once**, the other recordings are played twice. You mark your answers directly in the arkusz.

### 2. Rozumienie tekstów pisanych

Five tasks: multiple choice on short texts, TAK/NIE on a long text, inserting missing fragments, matching headings to paragraphs, and choosing the right word in a text (vocabulary).

### 3. Poprawność gramatyczna

Eight tasks, each on its own topic: declension of nouns, adjectives and pronouns; conjunctions; degrees of comparison; present and past tense; a question about the underlined part of a sentence; transforming a sentence with a given word; verb aspect and moods; prepositions. Most items are worth 0.5 points, transformations 1 point each. After this part **the arkusze are collected and there is a break**.

### 4. Pisanie

You have to write **two texts**: a short one (usually 20–50 words) and a long one (usually 150–180 words; sometimes a 60 + 140 variant). The arkusz has three zestawy, each with tasks *a* and *b*. You choose **one zestaw** and do both tasks from it; you cannot mix them. Each text is worth 15 points.

The grading uses three criteria, each from 0 to 10:

- **wykonanie zadania**: whether all the points of the task are covered and the form and length are respected;
- **środki językowe**: variety of words and constructions;
- **poprawność językowa**: grammar, spelling, punctuation.

Length is counted strictly: a deviation of about **±10%** is acceptable, and short words like *w* and *się* count as words too. It is best to split the long text into at least three paragraphs. You may cross things out, but do it neatly.

### 5. Mówienie

The oral part is held separately from the written parts (details below).

## The passing rule: 50% in every part

You pass the exam only if you **score at least 50% in each of the five parts**. A strong part does not carry a weak one: 28 out of 30 in grammar will not save 14 out of 30 in listening. So the main preparation strategy is always to bring up your weakest part, not your average score.

## The written and the oral part

The four written parts come as one block, in the order of the table, with a break after grammar. **Mówienie** is held separately and may be scheduled for a different day. The exact schedule comes from the exam centre.

How the written block works:

- you write your first and last name on every arkusz;
- when the time for a part is over, the signed arkusze are collected, and you cannot add anything afterwards;
- in listening, each recording is played as many times as the task instructions say: once or twice.

What to bring and what time to arrive: check the invitation from your centre.

## How Mówienie works

1. **Drawing lots.** You pick one zestaw out of three.
2. **Preparation: 5 minutes.** You may make notes, but only an outline, not a ready text.
3. **Answer: about 15 minutes.** At the start it is customary to introduce yourself briefly: this is not graded, but it is the first impression.

Each zestaw has three tasks:

- **Opis ilustracji**: describe a photo. First briefly what is in it, then the people (appearance, clothes, emotions, who they might be to each other), the place (*na pierwszym planie*, *w tle*), season and time of day, actions, overall impression.
- **Monolog**: a talk on a topic: personal experience, a description of a place, or an opinion with arguments. You need a structure: introduction, several aspects, conclusion.
- **Sytuacja komunikacyjna**: a dialogue with the examiner in a role: phone and arrange something, invite, persuade, solve a problem. You cannot guess the other person's reactions, so you prepare not lines but a plan: what your role is, what to ask, which three arguments to give.

What matters to the examiners:

- in tasks 1 and 2, use the *Pan / Pani* form; in the dialogue, use the one the role requires (*ty* with a friend, *Pan / Pani* in a formal situation);
- you can and should ask for clarification: *Przepraszam, czy może Pan powtórzyć?*;
- an accent is acceptable if it does not get in the way of understanding;
- speak enough, but do not drag it out: each task needs a beginning, a middle and an end.

## Tips for exam day

- **Cover all points of the polecenie.** A missed point costs more than a few grammar mistakes.
- **In Pisanie, choose the zestaw first**, looking at both tasks together, not just at the one you like.
- **Leave time to reread.** Count the words, check the greeting and the closing in the letter, and the commas before *że*, *bo*, *ponieważ*, *który*.
- **In listening, there is no second chance in the first task.** Mark your answer right away and move on.
- **While preparing for Mówienie, write only key words** and linking phrases: *Na początku…*, *Po pierwsze…*, *Po drugie…*, *Na koniec…*
- **Do not get stuck on one item.** In grammar and reading each item has its own point; skip a hard one and come back at the end.
- **Pace your energy.** The written block is long: after the break there is still a 75-minute Pisanie ahead.
