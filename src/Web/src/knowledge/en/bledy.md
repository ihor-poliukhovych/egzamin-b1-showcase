# Typical mistakes of Russian and Ukrainian speakers

> Fifteen mistakes that most often cost points at B1, each with the rule, an example and a quick self-check trick.

## How to use this

These mistakes do not come from not knowing the rule, but from the habit of your native language: in an exercise everything is correct, but in a text written under time pressure your hand writes "in Russian". That is why every item has a checking trick, a short action to do while rereading. Start with the three items where you recognise yourself.

## Grammar

### 1. Past tense for "I" and "we"

The person ending is required: *-em / -am* for "I", *-śmy* for "we". It is most often lost on the second verb in a sentence.

- ✗ *W sobotę poszli do kina i zjedli pizzę* (about yourself and friends) → ✓ *W sobotę poszliśmy do kina i zjedliśmy pizzę*
- **Check:** for every past-tense verb ask "who?". If it is "I" or "we", the ending must be visible.

### 2. Future tense of chcieć, móc, musieć

These verbs have no simple future: you need *będę / będziemy* + the form in *-ł*.

- ✗ *Jutro będę móc przyjść* → ✓ *Jutro będę mógł przyjść* · ✓ *będziemy musieli*
- **Check:** after *będę* with a modal verb, look for *-ł, -ła, -li, -ły*, not *-ć*.

### 3. Verb after dużo, wiele, kilka, 5+

After these words the verb is in the singular neuter, as if the subject were "it".

- ✗ *Dużo ludzi pracują w domu* → ✓ *Dużo ludzi pracuje w domu* · ✓ *Przyszło pięć osób*
- **Check:** is there a quantity before the verb? Then *pracuje, przyszło*. Exception: 2–4 take *Trzy osoby przyszły*.

### 4. The "on" and "wy" forms in the present tense

The "on" form is the "ty" form without *-sz*. The "wy" form is the same plus *-cie*.

- ✗ *Syn chodze do szkoły* → ✓ *Syn chodzi do szkoły* · ✗ *widziecie* → ✓ *widzicie*
- **Check:** say the "ty" form to yourself: *chodzisz → chodzi → chodzicie*.

### 5. Genitive after nie, coś, nic

Negation moves the object into the genitive. After *coś / nic* the adjective is in the genitive too.

- ✗ *Nie mam czas* → ✓ *Nie mam czasu* · ✗ *coś ważne* → ✓ *coś ważnego*
- **Check:** if you see *nie* before the verb, ask the question "kogo? czego?".

### 6. The verb być: the copula and the case

Polish does not drop the copula *jest / są*. After it the adjective is in the nominative, the noun in the instrumental.

- ✗ *Komputer bardzo szybki* → ✓ *Komputer jest bardzo szybki* · ✗ *Była odważną* → ✓ *Była odważna* · ✓ *Jest programistą*
- **Check:** find the verb in every sentence. If there is no action verb, you need the copula. But do not put it next to another verb: ✗ *Laptop jest działa*.

### 7. The preposition o: the verb decides the case

After *dbać, prosić, martwić się* it is *o + co?* (accusative). After *mówić, myśleć, rozmawiać* it is *o + czym?* (locative).

- ✗ *Martwię się o dzieciach* → ✓ *Martwię się o dzieci* · ✗ *Rozmawiamy o pracę* → ✓ *Rozmawiamy o pracy*
- **Check:** ask the question from the verb: "o co się martwię?", "o czym rozmawiamy?".

### 8. Men and everybody else

The forms *-li, którzy, ich, moi* are only for groups that include a man. For children, women, animals and things use *-ły, które, je, moje*.

- ✗ *Dzieci poszli do parku* → ✓ *Dzieci poszły do parku* · ✗ *Czekam na nich* (about days off) → ✓ *Czekam na nie*
- **Check:** ask yourself: is there a man in this group? If not, use the "non-masculine" form.

## Vocabulary and calques

### 9. Na or w with places

Open places, events and some districts take *na*. Direction and location are different: *do* is "to", *w* is "in", *z* is "from".

- ✗ *w osiedlu, do kempingu* → ✓ *na osiedlu, na kemping* · ✓ *idę do pracy — jestem w pracy — wracam z pracy*
- **Check:** learn such combinations in pairs: *na plaży, na siłowni, na basenie, na osiedlu*.

### 10. Verb government without a stray się

Russian constructions drag in *się* and the wrong prepositions.

- ✗ *korzystać się media* → ✓ *korzystać z mediów* · ✗ *dzwonić na numer* → ✓ *dzwonić pod numer*
- **Check:** write out the verbs with prepositions that you got wrong and compare against this list while rereading.

### 11. Word-for-word translation

The word looks like one in your native language, but means something else or is not said that way.

- ✗ *Pogoda jest jasna* → ✓ *Pogoda jest ładna* / *Jest słonecznie* · ✗ *doma* → ✓ *w domu*
- **Check:** if a phrase was "translated in your head", replace it with one you have already met in a Polish text.

## Spelling

### 12. Nasal ę and ą

Nasals at the end and inside a word get swallowed in writing, even though they change the form of the word.

- ✗ *Prosze, bede, mam nadzieje* → ✓ *Proszę, będę, mam nadzieję*
- **Check:** the "I" form in the present and future almost always ends in *-ę*: *proszę, mogę, będę*.

### 13. Soft consonants: ś or si

The marked letter (*ś, ć, ń, ź, dź*) is written before a consonant and at the end of a word; before a vowel, the combination with *i* is used.

- ✗ *muśiałem, ćocia* → ✓ *musiałem, ciocia* · ✓ *coś, być*
- **Check:** is there a vowel after the marked letter? Remove the mark and put an *i*.

### 14. Russian letters and endings

Sounds of the native language replace Polish letters.

- ✗ *chas, mażenie, pierwszyj, naszey, restaurasji* → ✓ *czas, marzenie, pierwszy, naszej, restauracji*
- **Check:** Russian «ч» is *cz*; where Russian has «р», Polish often has *rz*; the endings *-yj* and *-ey* do not exist in Polish; *-cja → -cji*, not *-sji*.

## Style and letter form

### 15. Greeting, register and required elements

In a letter the greeting is in the vocative case, and the register is kept to the end. A missing element of the form costs points for task completion.

- ✗ *Cześć Ania!* → ✓ *Cześć Aniu!* · ✗ *Szanowny Pan Kowalski* → ✓ *Szanowny Panie*
- ✓ Formal: *Pan / Pani* + 3rd person, closing with *Z poważaniem*. Informal: *ty*, closing with *Pozdrawiam*.
- ✓ An ogłoszenie must have a contact, a letter must have a closing and a signature; keep the length within ±10% of the norm.
- **Check:** before handing in, go through the checklist: greeting, all points of the task, closing, signature, word count. And separately the commas: needed before *że, bo, ponieważ, który, kiedy*, not needed before a single *i*.
